## Findings corrected

1. A dash had its own direction optimizer: target aim, an open heading and movement direction competed on hits versus landing crowd. The game executes the current aim. That optimizer is removed; 0.3 seconds of real invulnerability remain.
2. Ranged bot spacing was bounded by pickup-magnet distance. Attack reach now determines combat spacing; pickup collection remains a separate decision.
3. Contact was resolved during each enemy's movement, before body separation. Enemy movement and separation now precede attack casts; projectile resolution precedes contact. Fields, pickups, hazards and interactions follow in their own stages.
4. Non-pure hazard explosions previously respected existing dash/hit invulnerability. The official detonation clears it. That behavior and Damp's bomber-only exception are implemented.
5. The fixed projectile-collision arrays required at most 64 bodies and a boss deleted a regular enemy to make room. Collision storage is dynamic; a boss can enter above the normal spawn cap. An unsupported 80-player-projectile truncation was removed.
6. The current game's projectile defaults and upgrade ladders are imported. For example, Energy Ball's second level has two projectiles and spread 0.156, instead of silently failing its additive upgrade.

## New data and configurations

The Rust importer parses literal tables without executing JavaScript. The source checkout must be clean and exactly pinned. Output includes hashes of the upstream inputs. Reimporting the dataset reproduced `simulator/data/game.json` byte for byte.

The unchanged baseline attributes of all 966 species match the old extract. Enemy numerical definitions and behaviors match the old extract in all six tiers, which is expected: those definitions did not change in this patch. The new data adds the official nature, ability and TM tables.

Nature changes update physical/special attacks, defenses, speed and `atk=max(pa,sa)`. Ability and TM choices are validated against the species. The engine includes the current ability hooks and fallback primary-type bonus, and keeps the existing ×1.2 bonus for monotype own-type attacks. Traps and springs are represented for ability and movement interactions. The chosen second initial attack is tracked as already learned, separately from a future bond.

All JavaScript files and vendored game sources under `simulator/` were deleted, along with the Node-based game validator and its CI job. Only Rust implements and tests the simulator and imports its current data. JavaScript frontend tests elsewhere in the repository remain frontend tests.

## Search and historical isolation

The current Rust candidate generator uses natural moves plus compatible TMs, with every possible lead in the supported two/three-attack group shapes. It emits melee/ranged, aggro eligibility, own-type attack count and elemental resistance coverage. It does not require one move of each type or penalize Normal attacks. Nature/ability profile counts remain explicit; generating candidates is not optimization or measurement.

The generator reports 46,974 move groups for Mewtwo with 50 nature/ability profiles, and 9,776 groups for Psyduck with 75 profiles. These counts are enumeration results, not simulated results or performance claims.

Old ranking CLI commands and Python simulation writers are retired. Historical `--check` readers remain available and pass for attack, bond and species CSVs. The current engine cannot load the old schema. A diagnostic manifest requires the game commit and exact engine/data fingerprint; all its rows are validated before any run. It accepts at most 20 rows and at most 15 simulated seconds per row.

Existing public A/B builds and folded aggro/coverage variants remain the old snapshot. They have not been relabelled or regenerated with the new model. A future combined aggro+coverage recommendation must be evaluated as its own configuration.

## Limits before a full recalculation

No new scalar score or exposure weights have been invented. Raw diagnostics now expose damage received, contact attempts/time, surrounded time, longest surrounding episode, actual hit count, invulnerability time, dash casts and hits after dashes. The definition of surrounded time samples 16 body/terrain corridors and is diagnostic only.

Full-run comparison with the real game, calibration of exposure weights, validation of long-lived ability combinations, complete nature/ability profile selection, final build/variant selection and a new three-phase ranking remain subsequent work. Short runs and unit tests cannot establish those results. The old survival/DPS/kills score remains historical; it is not presented as the new method.
