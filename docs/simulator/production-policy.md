# Production simulator funnel

The production command builds a reproducible, bounded heuristic search plan. Planning executes no matches. `production plan simulator/runpod/production.json <plan.json>` validates the pinned game commit and engine fingerprint, creates a deterministic candidate roster, and writes a task budget before execution. `production run <plan.json> <cache-dir> <results.json>` starts it; `resume` uses the same plan and cache. Each completed match is an atomic JSON checkpoint identified by plan, configuration, stage, and seed. The internal wall-time limit is 900 seconds per invocation, so callers can resume after a budget stop. A cache lock prevents simultaneous writers.

The proposal generator does not enumerate every legal move group or profile. It constructs a capped attack beam, seeded across type, category, own-type, style, and natural/TM features, then proposes bounded lead/partner combinations and every legal single or paired buff from the retained pool. It keeps up to `groupsPerStratum` proposals for each dex/style/role/natural-or-TM stratum. The proposal score is a deterministic source-derived damage-per-cooldown proxy with physical/special fit, mono-type STAB, a quarter-power speed term, and the ability factors in `ranking_prior`: unconditional damage multipliers, the primary-type +8% when the ability has it, Hustle's expected hit, and incoming-damage reduction on the Base and Coverage roles only. Weather, low-HP, and on-kill triggers stay at 1, so they do not pretend to be active for the whole match. The planner expands every nature-stat class and every distinct ability, keeps the best nature of each ability, then retains at most `shortlistPerStratum` abilities. With the shipped shortlist of 1, one ability is simulated per stratum, chosen by that prior rather than by name order. IDs include move order, style, role, TM branch, nature class, ability, and a retained stock second start when relevant. No result is a global optimum.

The four role strata share the legal proposal universe. `Base` includes any group; `Aggro` admits an attack buff and excludes optional defensive buffs; `Coverage` requires at least two attack types; `Both` combines coverage and an attack buff while excluding defensive buffs. This is a proposal classification, not a claim that type count measures matchup coverage. Every retained candidate is simulated under the same match policy. Survival is the primary comparison; normalized contact damage per minute, contact-time fraction, and trapped-time fraction are separate safety constraints. The sample manifest uses zero margins: a candidate must be non-inferior to the fixed stock/no-TM control on survival and all three exposure measures. These defaults are deliberately conservative policy thresholds, not empirically calibrated game-balance weights.

All candidates and their fixed stock controls receive paired screening seeds. Within each stratum, screening results select at most `confirmPerStratum` candidates by paired survival difference among candidates whose *screening means* meet all exposure margins. If none meet those margins, the highest-survival screening candidate is still confirmed so the output can report a measured unsafe result. Confirmation seeds are disjoint and are not used in nomination. The plan stores conditional confirmation templates and a worst-case task ceiling; execution may create confirmation tasks only for listed candidate IDs and never above those templates or the ceiling. An incomplete screen produces no confirmations for that stratum.

The final report uses paired Student-t 95% descriptive intervals, with a conventional large-sample critical value beyond 30 degrees of freedom. The intervals are unadjusted for the many candidate comparisons, so they are descriptive and do not establish statistical superiority. Fewer than two paired confirmation seeds leaves uncertainty unresolved. `winnerConfirmed` means independent confirmation completed and all safety/non-inferiority interval bounds passed; it does not mean a statistically unique winner. `best_observed` names the highest observed survival among confirmed safe candidates; `tied_best` means its survival interval overlaps the best-observed candidate interval. `no_safe_candidate` and `inconclusive` remain explicit outcomes. Search-selected candidates and source-feature coverage cannot support a global ranking claim.

`policyHash` hashes the manifest's `searchPolicy`; `manifestHash` hashes the canonical complete manifest; `planHash` additionally binds the candidate roster, task templates, game commit, and engine fingerprint. Plan execution rechecks these values and the Rust compiler version. The result contains observations, paired seed rows, winners, missing tasks, unavailable strata, limits, and provenance for independent export.

For CPU sizing only, `production benchmark <dex> <seed> <seconds<=15> <foes:10|20|30> <threads> <repetitions<=20> [mature]` runs independent scripted engine scenes in a bounded Rayon pool. It reports aggregate simulated seconds per wall second, per-scene timings, host metadata, and the exact mature-fixture label. These scenes do not model the full spawn schedule and are not ranking evidence. Measure on the target host; do not extrapolate a universal runtime or cost from one machine.

## Funnel v1 (provisional until gate 2)

`pokesim funnel` is the morning search. The production beam above still plans and runs. It is not the search the morning Pod should execute. `rankingReady` stays false until gate 2.

Four builds when the species has a legal TM. Melee and ranged, each without a taught TM and with at least one. Nineteen species have no legal TM and keep only the two no-TM builds. Their dex numbers are 10, 11, 13, 14, 129, 132, 201, 202, 235, 265, 266, 268, 360, 374, 412, 789, 790, 824, and 840. A TM build is still reported when it loses, with the paired difference and the CI. Aggro, coverage, and both stay out of the search. They are web controls.

`tmCost` sums the shop prices of TMs that must be taught. A move already in the natural pool, or the stock start, costs 0. `tmBudget` is optional and has no default. Omitting it and setting it to null produce the same plan hash. A number filters the roster at plan time and is checked again on the confirmed row.

The objective is mean paired survival against the stock no-TM control. The exposure margins used for `safe` are 10% of the control value. The report also gives the no_safe_candidate rate at 0%, 5%, and 10%. Those fractions reclassify the single stage-D candidate. They do not rerun the search.

Recall passes when the CI95 upper bound of (real best minus funnel best), on seeds 3001 through 3032, is at most 5% of the real best's survival. Recall enumerates every legal group at hardy and the slot-1 ability on seeds 1001 through 1004, then re-measures the top 50 per style and branch on the confirmation seeds. The funnel pick in that comparison is the best member of the stage A shortlist on the four screening seeds.

A legal opener is the game's `startChoices` at ce57853. The set is the species' own type pools, plus Normal, plus a TM taught on that build, plus the stock start even when it is off-type. Confusion on Psyduck is that stock start. A TM opener is emitted only on the TM branch and counts in `tmCost`. The funnel's `build_config` check and `build/funnel_export.py` `legal_initial` use that same set. `build/sim_results.py` accepts the same leads when it validates a published document. The recorded second start has to differ from the first.

Stage A enumerates every legal group for a species and branch when that count is at most 5932. The cap is the largest distinct group count whose morning ledger stays inside 12,323,520 tasks. That ceiling is 2.5 hours at 1369.28 matches per second, the 32-vCPU rate inferred from this VM. On this roster, 1871 strata are fully enumerated (2,524,304 groups) and 1127 strata fall back to the prior shortlist of 30 (33,810 groups). The prior's move beam of 24, one move per type, category, STAB, melee, and TM combination, applies only on that fallback. Stages B and C stay at 4 and 2 groups. The dry-run upper bound is 12,061,932 tasks, 2.447 hours and 2.74 USD at 1.12 USD per hour. On that plan, 0 of 2,558,114 stage A candidates have an opener outside the set, and 0 of 2,998 strata have no legal opener.

Harden (Fortaleza) and Agility are optional buffs on a shorter attack list. `visit_groups` keeps the attack-only group and appends a buff copy when the attack list is shorter than 4. A group of four attacks is left unchanged. The same rule is what `each_legal_group` uses under the cap. `main.rs` line 1037 names a kit for the old research sweep. It appends the string agility or harden only when that kit's buff bit is set. The funnel does not call that sweep. A status move is in a published build when that group wins on survival.
